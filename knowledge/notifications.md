# Notifications

Push notifications are delivered via APNs (iOS) and FCM (Android). If the OS-level permission is denied, the client cannot register a device token and no pushes are delivered.

To turn notifications on: open your phone's Settings, find the app, tap Notifications and switch on Allow Notifications. Then open the app and go to Profile > Notifications to choose which reminders you get.

Daily reminders are sent at 09:00 in your phone's time zone. You can change the time under Profile > Notifications > Reminder time.
