# Exporting or deleting your data

Under GDPR Article 15 and 20, users may request a machine-readable export. Go to Profile > Privacy > Export my data. An async job compiles a JSON archive and emails a signed download link, valid for 7 days. Export usually arrives within 24 hours.

To delete your account, go to Profile > Privacy > Delete account and confirm. Deletion is a soft delete for 30 days, during which you can log back in to cancel. After 30 days all personal data is permanently erased and cannot be restored.
