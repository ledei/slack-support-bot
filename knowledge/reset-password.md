# Resetting your password

If you forgot your password, open the login page and click "Forgot password".

Enter the email address you use for your account. We send you a reset link within 5 minutes. Check your spam folder if you do not see it.

The reset link works for 1 hour. After that, request a new one.

If your company uses single sign-on (SSO), you cannot reset your password here. Ask your company's IT team instead.
